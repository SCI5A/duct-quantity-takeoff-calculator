# Calculation Methods

Rectangular duct surface = 2 × (W + H) × L
Round duct surface = π × D × L
Sheet weight = area × thickness(m) × 7850 kg/m³

Insulation = external duct surface × (1 + insulation waste)
Adhesive = insulation area × adhesive rate
Tape = insulation area × tape rate

G-Flange = perimeter × joints × 2
Rectangular flange corners = 4 × joints × 2
Gasket = joint perimeter × 2
Cleats = ceil(joint perimeter / cleat spacing)
Silicone tubes = ceil(joint perimeter / tube coverage)
Bolts/Nuts = cleats

These are takeoff estimates and must be checked against project specifications and manufacturer data.
